Who & Where

En — meaning grace

En's Pâtisserie is a small home-based bakery born from one simple wish: that the people we love shouldn't have to give up cake. Everything is baked to order, by hand, with ingredients we'd happily feed our own family.

Where to find us

Home-based bakery in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Self pick-up by appointment, with delivery available around the Klang Valley.

Ordering

Kindly place orders at least 3 days in advance. Custom flavours, sizes and dietary requests are always welcome.

What makes it different

No refined sugar, no wheat flour. Keto, low-carb and diabetic friendly — without tasting like it.

Hours

Monday to Saturday, 10am – 7pm. Messages are answered as soon as the oven allows.
